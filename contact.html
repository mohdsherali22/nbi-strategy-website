Common Questions

Before you ask

How long does it take to build?

A focused workflow automation usually ships in days. A chatbot or knowledge assistant takes one to two weeks including training on your documents. A digital employee — with memory, skills, channels and approvals — typically takes two to four weeks including testing. You get a dated plan in the first week and progress updates throughout.

Do we need our own AI accounts or API keys?

Either works. We can configure the model providers and manage keys for you, or set everything up under your own accounts so billing and data agreements stay in your name. For self-hosted deployments we always configure credentials securely on your side, and we'll recommend the model provider that fits your budget and privacy needs.

Where does our data live?

Your choice. Solutions can run on managed cloud infrastructure, or fully self-hosted on your own computer, server or VPS in a Docker-based environment. Every deployment ships with security controls as standard — pairing and allowlists, sandboxing, permission controls, secure credential storage, and human approval before sensitive actions.

Can the AI work on WhatsApp and in our language?

Yes. Agents and assistants can be reached through WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, your website and by voice — and modern AI models converse fluently in most major languages. Multilingual support is configured and tested as part of the build, not bolted on later.

What does an engagement cost?

Builds are fixed-fee, quoted after the scoping call, with any third-party costs (model usage, hosting, tool subscriptions) itemized separately so you see exactly what goes where. Ongoing care — monitoring, improvements, new skills — runs on a monthly plan sized to what we manage. No engagement starts without a written, capped quote.

What happens after launch?

Every deployment includes handover and training, activity logs you can inspect, automated backups and health monitoring. Most clients keep a care plan under which we watch the logs, fix what breaks, and ship improvements monthly — but everything we build is documented and yours, so you're never locked in.

Prefer to just talk it through?

Call us during office hours, or message us on WhatsApp any time — we'll reply quickly and hand you to a consultant the moment you need one.
