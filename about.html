The team businesses call when AI has to actually work.

NBI Strategy was founded on a simple observation: businesses don't miss out on AI because the technology isn't ready — they miss out because nobody owns the outcome. We built a studio that owns it.

Our Story

From automated workflows to digital employees

We started by automating the unglamorous work — lead follow-ups, reports, onboarding — and watched small teams win back whole days every week. The same principle runs the studio today: ship something useful fast, measure it, then earn the right to ambition.

Today we build across the full spectrum — workflow automation, knowledge assistants, voice agents and fully-fledged digital employees on platforms like Hermes and OpenClaw — for startups, agencies and established businesses alike. Every build ships with security controls, activity logs and a human in the loop where it matters.

What We Stand For

Principles that survive contact with production

Ownership, not demos

We don't hand you a proof-of-concept and wish you luck. We deploy, monitor, fix and improve — the outcome stays ours until it works.

Boring wins first

The best AI project is the one that pays for itself in a month. We start with invoices, inboxes and follow-ups — not moonshots.

Private by design

Allowlists, sandboxing, permission controls and human approval before sensitive actions — in every deployment, hosted or self-hosted.
